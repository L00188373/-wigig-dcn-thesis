╔════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║      Comparison with Wired Technologies               ║
╚════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

Technology          Throughput    Latency    Packet Loss    Cabling
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Cat5/6 Ethernet     1,000 Mbps    <1 ms      <0.01%         Required
60 GHz WiGig        2,700 Mbps    60-90 ms   ~70%           None
10G Fiber           10,000 Mbps   <0.5 ms    <0.01%         Required

Key Findings:
  • WiGig provides 2.7x the speed of Cat6 Ethernet
  • WiGig achieves 27% of 10G Fiber performance
  • No physical cabling required for WiGig
  • Best use: Backup links, flexible connections, temporary deployments
